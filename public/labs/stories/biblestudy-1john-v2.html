Background

The Fight for Truth

John wrote this letter late in the first century to combat a dangerous heresy (early Gnosticism) that claimed spirit was good but matter (the body) was evil.
👻

The Lie

"Jesus didn't have a real body. Sin doesn't matter because it's only physical."

🩸

John's Response

"We heard, we saw, we touched Him." (1:1). The Incarnation is real. How we live in the body matters.

Reflection

Do you treat your faith as merely a mental belief, or a tangible reality that affects your physical life?

1 John 2:15-17

Do Not Love the World

The "World" isn't nature; it's the system opposed to God. It has three traps:

Lust of the Flesh 🍖

Cravings for physical pleasure outside God's bounds.

Lust of the Eyes 👀

Greed, materialism, and coveting what we see.

Pride of Life 👑

Arrogance about status, wealth, or achievements.

"The world is passing away along with its desires, but whoever does the will of God abides forever."

👑

Behold!

"See what kind of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God; and so we are."

The Purifying Hope:

If you really believe you are royalty (God's child) and will see Him soon, you won't play in the mud (sin).

Identity drives behavior.
