Friend or Foe?

Not all inflammation is bad.

🚑

Acute (The Good)

You cut your finger. It gets red, hot, and swollen. This is your immune system sending "firefighters" to kill bacteria and heal the wound. It goes away in days.

👺

Chronic (The Bad)

The body thinks it's under constant attack. The "firefighters" don't leave. They start damaging healthy tissues, organs, and DNA. It lasts for years.

Think of it this way:
Acute is a controlled campfire. Chronic is a forest fire that never goes out.

It's often invisible until it's too late.

🧠

😴

🤕
