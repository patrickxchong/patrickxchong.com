Bangkok 4-Day Budget Itinerary (13–16 Nov)

Each day includes attractions, food highlights, travel tips, and an embedded map.

Day 1: Lumpini Park – Siam – Chinatown

  • Morning: Lumpini Park (free), early walk, coconut ice cream (~THB50, RM7)
  • Midday: Bangkok Art & Culture Centre (free), MBK/Siam lunch (~THB50–60, RM7–9)
  • Evening: Chinatown street food tour (noodle soup, satay, dim sum ~THB100 total, RM15)
  • Travel: BTS/MRT (total ~THB100/day, RM15); use Rabbit card or cash
  • ⚠️ Crowds in Chinatown peak after 7pm; keep bags secure.

Day 2: Bang Krachao – Terminal21 – Ratchada

  • Morning: Bang Krachao ferry + bike (~THB150, RM22), nature ride
  • Afternoon: Terminal21 food court (basil rice, green curry ~THB60, RM9)
  • Evening: Ratchada Train Market – seafood, mango sticky rice (~THB120, RM18)
  • Travel: BTS + boat (~THB100 total, RM15); cash or QR for taxi/motorbike
  • ⚠️ Few shops in Bang Krachao – bring water and bug spray

Day 3: Chatuchak Market – Victory Monument

  • Morning: Chatuchak Market (ice cream, boat noodles, souvenirs ~THB100–150, RM15–22)
  • Afternoon: Chatuchak Park or JJ Mall rest; drinks (~THB30, RM5)
  • Evening: Boat noodles at Victory Monument (~THB40, RM6)
  • Travel: BTS/MRT (~THB100/day, RM15); cash for most vendors
  • ⚠️ Watch for pickpockets; wear a backpack in front.

Day 4: Final Walk – Lumpini or Riverfront

  • Morning: Optional revisit to Lumpini Park or Wang Lang Market
  • Afternoon: Terminal21 or MBK for last meal (~THB60–100, RM9–15)
  • Travel: Keep costs low with BTS/MRT (~THB80, RM12)
  • ⚠️ Some malls and shops close earlier on Sundays
