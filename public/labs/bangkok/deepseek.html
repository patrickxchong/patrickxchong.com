Day 1: Thursday

Arrival & Local Exploration (Ratchaprasong Area)

Time Activity Food to Try Budget & Transport
2:00 PM Check-in & Settle - -
3:00 PM Erawan Shrine - Free Hindu shrine with cultural performances - Free. Walk from hotel.
4:00 PM CentralWorld & Siam Paragon - Explore malls & food courts Som Tum Khao Soi Food: ฿150-250 (RM 20-32). Walk or 1 BTS stop to Siam.
5:30 PM Lumpini Park - Bangkok's "Green Lung", watch monitor lizards - Free. Walk from hotel.
7:30 PM Patpong Night Market - Famous market (be prepared for touts) - Free to browse. BTS to Sala Daeng.
8:30 PM Dinner at Convent Road - Local eateries away from market chaos Kuay Teow Reua Moo Ping Food: ฿120-200 (RM 15-26). Walk from Patpong.
Daily Estimated Spend: ฿470 (RM 61)
Map: Hotel → Erawan Shrine → CentralWorld → Siam Paragon → Lumpini Park → Patpong Night Market → Convent Road

Day 2: Friday

Markets, Culture & Jazz

Time Activity Food to Try Budget & Transport
9:00 AM Chatuchak Weekend Market - One of the world's largest markets Coconut Ice Cream Grilled Squid Thai-style Sausages Free entry. BTS to Mo Chit. Food/Drink: ฿200 (RM 26).
1:00 PM Or Tor Kor Market - Gourmet fresh market next to Chatuchak Mango Sticky Rice Grilled Prawns Thai Desserts Food: ฿250 (RM 32). Walk from Chatuchak.
3:00 PM Jim Thompson House - Traditional Thai house & silk museum - Entry: ฿200 (RM 26). BTS to National Stadium.
7:00 PM Dinner in Victory Monument - Area known for boat noodle alley Boat Noodles Food: ฿150 (RM 20). BTS to Victory Monument.
9:00 PM Saxophone Pub - Legendary live music bar (blues, jazz, R&B) - 1 Drink: ฿200+ (RM 26+). BTS to Victory Monument.
Daily Estimated Spend: ฿1,000 (RM 130)

Note: This is over your lower budget due to the Jim Thompson entry and jazz pub, but you can adjust by skipping Jim Thompson.

Map: Hotel → Chatuchak Market → Or Tor Kor Market → Jim Thompson House → Victory Monument → Saxophone Pub

Day 3: Saturday

Old City & River Crossings

Time Activity Food to Try Budget & Transport
8:00 AM View Wat Arun from Across the River - Free view of Temple of Dawn - Transport: ฿30 (RM 4). BTS to Saphan Taksin, then public ferry.
10:00 AM Talad Phlu (Phlu Market) - Local, non-touristy market Khanom Bueang Hoy Tod Food: ฿150 (RM 20). BTS to Talad Phlu.
1:00 PM Free Time / Rest - Escape the midday heat - -
5:00 PM Talad Rot Fai Srinakarin - Original "Train Market" with vintage section Suki Haeng Lobster Noodles Free entry. Taxi/Bolt from BTS Udom Suk (฿100-150). Food: ฿300 (RM 39).
9:00 PM The Roof @ 38th Bar - Affordable rooftop view - 1 Drink: ฿250 (RM 32). BTS to Phrom Phong.
Daily Estimated Spend: ฿1,030 (RM 134)

Note: Transport cost is higher due to taxi to Rot Fai. Split with friends to reduce cost.

Map: Hotel → Wat Arun Viewpoint → Talad Phlu → Talad Rot Fai Srinakarin → The Roof @ 38th Bar

Day 4: Sunday

Green Space & Last-Minute Souvenirs

Time Activity Food to Try Budget & Transport
8:00 AM Bang Krachao - "Bangkok's Green Lung", cycling paths - Bike Rental: ฿100 (RM 13). BTS to Bang Na, then taxi/motorcycle to pier, boat (฿10).
12:00 PM Lunch in Bang Krachao - Riverside restaurant Pla Pao Som Tum Lunch: ฿200 (RM 26).
2:00 PM Big C Supercenter Ratchadamri - Last-minute souvenir shopping - Souvenirs: Your budget. Walk from hotel.
4:00 PM Head to Airport - Transport: ฿45 (RM 6). BTS to Phaya Thai, then Airport Rail Link.
Daily Estimated Spend: ฿555 (RM 72) + Souvenirs
