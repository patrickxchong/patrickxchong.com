Section 1: Clothing & accessories

Coconut ice cream - ฿30-40 (RM4-5.50)
Grilled squid - ฿50-80 (RM7-11)
Fresh coconut juice - ฿20-30 (RM2.50-4)
Mango sticky rice - ฿60-80 (RM8-11)
Pad see ew - ฿40-60 (RM5.50-8)

💡 Essential Tips & Info

Everything you need to know for a smooth trip

🚇 Transport Guide

Rabbit Card (BTS)

💳 Cost: ฿200 (฿100 deposit + ฿100 credit)
📍 Buy at: Any BTS station (get yours at Chit Lom on Day 1)
💰 Saves: ฿2-5 per trip, skip ticket queues
⚡ Top-up: 7-Eleven or BTS stations

Cheap Transport Options (Ranked)

1. BTS/MRT: ฿16-44/trip (RM2-6) - Most reliable
2. Orange Flag Boat: ฿15-20/trip (RM2-2.50) - Scenic!
3. Public Bus: ฿8-15 (RM1-2) - Hot, crowded, slow
4. Grab (Share): ฿40-100 (RM5.50-13) - Split with friends
5. Walking: FREE - Use Google Maps offline

📱 Essential Apps

📍 Google Maps: Download offline Bangkok map
🗣️ Google Translate: Download Thai language offline
🚗 Grab/Bolt: Transport apps (Bolt often cheaper)
💱 XE Currency: Real-time exchange rates
🚌 ViaBus: Bangkok bus routes
🗺️ Moovit: Public transport planning

⚠️ Important Warnings

Weather (Mid-November)

🌡️ Temperature: 25-33°C (77-91°F)
💧 Humidity: 70-80%
🌧️ Rain: Possible short showers
🎒 Bring: Umbrella, water bottle, sunscreen, hat, light clothes

Crowd Warnings

🚨 WORST crowds: Chatuchak 11am-3pm Sat/Sun
🏮 Chinatown: Crazy in evenings, go morning instead
🚇 BTS/MRT rush hours: 7-9am, 5-7pm

🛡️ Safety Tips

👜 Pickpockets: Markets & crowded BTS - keep valuables in front pockets
🚕 Scams: Tuk-tuk drivers near tourist spots - politely ignore
🍜 Food safety: Eat where locals eat, avoid empty stalls
🌡️ Heat exhaustion: Take AC breaks every 2 hours, hydrate constantly
💧 Water: Bottled water only, available everywhere (฿7-10)
🚻 Bathrooms: 7-Eleven has clean toilets (small purchase expected)

💰 Money Tips

🏦 ATM: Use Big C or 7-Eleven ATMs (lower fees)
💵 Small bills: Keep ฿20, ฿50, ฿100 for street food
💳 Notify bank: Tell your bank you're traveling to Thailand
🚫 Large bills: Markets rarely have change for ฿1000 notes
📊 Cash vs Card: 70% cash, 30% card/QR in Bangkok

🎵 Jazz Scene Notes

🍺 Most Bangkok jazz venues serve alcohol
🎸 Parking Toys (Thonglor): Jazz cafe with non-alcohol options
☀️ Sunday Jazz Brunch: Various hotels (check current schedules)
🎭 FREE alternative: Street performers at Siam Square, CentralWorld plaza (especially weekends)
📞 Pro tip: Call venues ahead to confirm schedule

🌟 Pro Traveler Final Tips

⏰ Golden rule: Start early (8am) before heat and crowds
💧 Hydration: Buy big water bottles at 7-Eleven (฿15 for 1.5L)
🎒 Day bag essentials: Water, umbrella, tissues, small bills, phone charger
📸 Photos: Ask before photographing street vendors (usually okay with smile)
🙏 Thai greeting: "Sawasdee krap" (male) / "Sawasdee ka" (female)
🍴 Food rule: If locals are lining up, join them!
❄️ AC breaks: 7-Eleven, malls, BTS stations when you need to cool down
📱 Free WiFi: Most malls and cafes, use for Grab/maps
